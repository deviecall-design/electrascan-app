# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Electrical contractors and estimators — solo electricians or small electrical contracting businesses running their own takeoffs and bids. They work at a desk preparing quotes, and sometimes on-site with a laptop or tablet referencing plans in bright, uncontrolled light.

## Product Purpose

ElectraScan turns PDF blueprints into accurate, professional bids faster than manual takeoff and spreadsheet estimating. Success means less time spent measuring and pricing, and fewer errors making it into a submitted quote.

## Positioning

ElectraScan closes the full loop from takeoff to order: measure the plan, build the estimate, generate the bill of materials, send it to a wholesaler for pricing, and track it through to an ordered job. Competing tools stop at a printed quote; ElectraScan keeps going until the materials are on order.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, friendly, low-friction. The tool should feel like it respects a tradesperson's time — plain language, no jargon-heavy toolbars, nothing that requires training to open and use.

## Anti-references

Bloated legacy enterprise/construction ERP software: dense toolbars, dated skeuomorphic chrome, overwhelming menus, and interfaces that assume a dedicated power user rather than a busy contractor.

## Design Principles

- **Close the loop, don't just estimate.** Every workflow should visibly connect takeoff to the ordered materials, not stop at a printed quote — the bid-to-order chain is the product's whole reason to exist.
- **Approachable over enterprise.** Favor plain language and low-friction flows over dense toolbars and specialist jargon; a first-time user should be productive without training.
- **Jobsite-legible.** Design for bright, uncontrolled light and non-precise input: strong contrast, generous touch targets, and no affordance that depends on a hover state.
- **Accuracy is the product.** Precision in numbers and clarity in how costs and quantities are presented is non-negotiable — the interface must never introduce ambiguity into a bid.
- **Fast and unencumbered.** Respect that estimators want speed in the moment; local-first performance and low friction beat feature depth.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Baseline WCAG AA plus jobsite conditions: contrast should comfortably clear AA (favor the higher end of the range) since screens may be viewed outdoors or in bright light. Touch targets sized generously for tablet/laptop use in the field. Avoid interactions that rely solely on hover.
